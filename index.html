Module 1: Phishing & Social Engineering

Email Phishing is an attack that utilizes "harmless" emails to trick you into revealing sensitve information,downloading malware, or sending money

Display Name vs Sender Address

Attackers often set the display name of an email to a trusted brand while using an unrelated or compromised domain

Domain TypoSquatting

Registering domains that look almost identical to legitimate ones, such as replacing letters with similar looking characters

Messages are crafted to generate panic or extreme urgency to force quick, descisions on the victim removing the critical thinking aspect

Lesson 2: Passwords & Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

Strong passwords are essential for protecting your online accounts. Use a combination of uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters. Avoid using easily guessable information like birthdays or common words.

Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) adds an extra layer of security by requiring users to provide two or more verification factors to gain access to an account. This can include something you know (password), something you have (security token), or something you are (biometric verification).
